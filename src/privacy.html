Funny Jump – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11

Funny Jump (web game, Chrome extension and Android app) does not collect, sell or share personal data.

  • Your progress (coins, levels, settings) is saved only on your own device using the browser's local storage.
  • No analytics, ads, cookies or trackers.
  • Online leaderboard (optional): only if you press “Submit score”, the nickname you type, your Endless score and distance are sent to our leaderboard database (Supabase) and shown publicly in the World Top 20. Don't use your real name if you don't want it public. Nothing else is sent. The leaderboard is also read (top scores only) when you open the Trophies screen.
  • The Chrome extension requests no permissions and cannot read your tabs or browsing data.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing site data deletes all saved progress.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/nikhilrana2715/Running-Game.
